How We Help

At Twin Cities Animal Rescue, we work to give animals a second chance through adoption, fostering, and the support of dedicated volunteers. There are many ways to get involved and make a difference in the life of an animal in need.

Adoption Process

Adopting a pet is an exciting decision, and our goal is to help animals find safe and loving homes that are a good fit for them.

  1. Learn about the animals currently available for adoption.
  2. Submit an adoption inquiry for the animal you are interested in.
  3. Talk with a rescue team member about your home and lifestyle.
  4. Meet the animal and ask any questions you may have.
  5. Complete the adoption process and welcome your new pet home.

Foster Program

Foster families provide temporary homes for animals while they wait for adoption. Fostering gives animals a safe and comfortable environment and helps them adjust to life in a home. It also gives our rescue more space to help additional animals in the community.

Foster homes may care for animals for different lengths of time depending on the animal's needs. Twin Cities Animal Rescue works with foster families to help them prepare for the responsibility and provide the care each animal needs.

Volunteer With Us

Volunteers are an important part of our rescue community. Whether you have a little time or want to become regularly involved, your help can make a meaningful difference.

Volunteer Opportunities

  • Help care for and socialize rescued animals.
  • Assist with adoption and community events.
  • Help organize supplies and donations.
  • Support outreach and animal rescue awareness efforts.

Interested in volunteering or fostering? Contact us to get started.
