Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue was created to help animals in need find safety, care, and loving homes. Our work brings together volunteers, foster families, adopters, donors, and community partners who share the belief that every animal deserves a second chance.

Through rescue, fostering, adoption, and community involvement, we work to connect animals with people who can give them the care and stability they deserve. Every successful adoption begins with people in the community choosing to help.

Our Core Values

  • Compassion: We treat every animal with patience, kindness, and respect.
  • Responsibility: We work to make thoughtful decisions that support the well-being of the animals in our care.
  • Community: We believe lasting change is possible when people work together to help animals in need.
  • Commitment: We are committed to helping rescued animals move toward safe and loving permanent homes.

Working Together

Our community partners play an important role in helping us continue our mission. We are grateful for the support of local veterinary professionals, animal shelters, businesses, volunteers, foster families, donors, and other organizations that help make our rescue efforts possible.

These partnerships provide valuable resources, care, supplies, outreach, and support for animals while they wait for their forever homes.
