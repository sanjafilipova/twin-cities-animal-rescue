Helping Animals Find Their Way Home

Our mission at Twin Cities Animal Rescue is to give animals in need a second chance by connecting them with safe, loving homes and caring people in our community. Through adoption, fostering, volunteering, and community support, we work to make a difference in the lives of animals one step at a time.

Our Impact

Each year, our volunteers and foster families help animals receive the care, attention, and support they need while waiting for their permanent homes.

  • Hundreds of animals supported through rescue and adoption efforts
  • Dozens of foster families providing temporary, caring homes
  • Community volunteers helping animals throughout the year

Meet an Adoptable Pet

Animals of all shapes and sizes can need a new home. Our adoptable pets are looking for people who can provide the care, patience, and love they deserve.

How You Can Help

There are many ways to support Twin Cities Animal Rescue. Whether you are ready to adopt, can temporarily foster an animal, have time to volunteer, or simply want to support our work, every contribution can make a difference.

  • Adopt an animal and provide a permanent home.
  • Become a foster and provide temporary care for an animal in need.
  • Volunteer your time to support animals and rescue activities.
  • Support our mission and help spread awareness in the community.

Contact us to learn how you can get involved.
